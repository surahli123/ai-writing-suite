## Findings (most severe first)

**1. [blocker, step 9 §D] The praise-overlap rule misses its own motivating example.** VERIFIED. Plan: "Overlap means one contains the other after normalization." Probe: `norm("The dashboard is fast") in norm("fast, clean, and intuitive")` is False in both directions. The double-dip in `skills/ai-writing-suite/skills/comms-polish/references/audit-report-format.md:50,64` and `skills/ai-writing-suite/evals/audit_report/conforming.md:18,32` is *sentence-level* (both snippets sit in draft sentence 3), which containment cannot see. Fix: when `draft` is given, map each snippet to the draft sentence(s) it falls in and reject any strength whose sentence set intersects a finding's; without a draft, fall back to containment with a minimum normalized length (≥3 tokens) so "the team" vs "the team shipped Friday" is not a false rejection. The planted overlap in `nonconforming.md` must then be a true containment case, or the test must pass the draft.

**2. [major, §E] The ×N count cannot be checked from the current parser, and the snippet grammar is undefined.** VERIFIED. `evals/audit_report/check_report.py:50` `_FIELD_LINE` captures `(.*)$` on the marker line only; `_check_one_finding` (157–160) ignores continuation lines, so a Quote line wrapped across lines loses snippets and ×N rejects a correct report. Nested quotes (a draft that itself quotes: `"He called it "fast""`) make pair-splitting ambiguous. Fix: define the multi-instance form as one sub-bullet per instance under `**Quote:**` (or join continuation lines until the next field marker) and anchor `×N` at the start of Why (a Why saying "claims a x10 gain" must not trigger). Define "instance" (per sentence? the F1 finding in `conforming.md:26` quotes one sentence with two dashes).

**3. [major, §E] Verbatim-substring normalization is under-specified.** VERIFIED by probe: with quote-style + whitespace normalization only, `It's` ≠ `It’s` and `—` ≠ `--`; `_QUOTE_PAIRS` (`check_report.py:68`) covers double quotes and guillemets, not apostrophes or dashes. Paste-from-Word drafts carry `’`. Also markdown: a report quoting `Shipped:` from a draft's `**Shipped:**`. Fix: normalize apostrophes, dashes, strip `*`/`_`/backticks, collapse whitespace, compare case-insensitively (case buys nothing against a hallucinated quote), and for the open ellipsis question: split on `…`/`...`, require each part ≥ ~12 chars and in order.

**4. [major, §B] "Prefer verb-first … only when the actor is already stated or doesn't matter" licenses dropping ownership.** VERIFIED conflict: `references/final-pass-checklist.md:22` "The author's stance, attributions, and commitments survive"; `comms-polish/SKILL.md:122` "Never move an attributed claim onto the author." In a status list, who owns an item is a fact. Fix: drop the actor only when the heading or lead-in states it; delete "or doesn't matter."

**5. [major, §A/B] The parallel-set trigger is circular and fires in the default mode.** JUDGMENT. "Siblings a reader skims as a set" + "leave alone: mixed lists" defines mixed by the same judgment. `rewrite` is the fallback when the mode is unclear (`comms-polish/SKILL.md:82`), so a memo's three heterogeneous bullets (decision / risk / ask) become candidates for one frame. Fix: parallel set = ≥3 items of one *kind* under one heading or lead-in (all statuses, all titles, all metric rows, all action items); "mixed" = items answering different questions. "A shared prefix moves into the heading" only when a heading/lead-in already exists; never add one (a new lead-in is added content). Held-out items h-04 and h-20 (`notes/eval-lift-2026-11-01/v1/run1/items_heldout.json`) are label-first lists and already score S=0/W=1.0 for s8, main and bare (run2 `report.md` by-category table), so the risk here is new harm, not a measured gain.

## Answers to the seven questions

1. **T7 reframe:** yes, if "checkable" is defined as a verifiable thing (finding 6); as written, "object" lets "Robust performance" through. Tier-1 boast lists are still caught by L1 regardless.
2. **"Three or more siblings":** usable only with the one-kind-under-one-heading definition (finding 5). As written it fires on ordinary heterogeneous bullets in the default `rewrite` mode.
3. **D/E:** substring overlap is the wrong test (finding 1); sentence-level mapping against the draft is right, containment is only the no-draft fallback. The ×N rule is checkable without false rejections only after fixing the parser and defining the snippet grammar (finding 2) and normalization (finding 3).
4. **Widening `Notes:`:** safe (judges hide notes; rewrite text unchanged), and already needed by step 6 and R1; enumerate the allowed classes (finding 15).
5. **One skill, two entry points:** right call. The router will confuse them unless the boundary is "how it reads" vs "whether the argument holds," with near-miss cases both ways (finding 13).
6. **Simulated-author eval:** not valid for a ship verdict as designed; the missing bare-staged arm and the asymmetry in author turns are the problems (finding 11). Cheapest design: 8 packets × 3 arms × K=1 with four deterministic measures and two judged ones, plus the owner's 2–3 real documents.
7. **Missing:** findings 8, 9, 14, 20, 21 (pins and files); no owner ruling is violated, but R4's "second or third word" is a manufactured-perplexity lever the plan leaves in place (finding 19), and the "≥3 siblings / ≥2 share a slot" triggers are numeric scope tests the owner should see named as such.
